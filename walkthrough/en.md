# RoomBreak Escape Now — Complete Walkthrough

This guide follows the six chapters on the stage selection screen and covers all thirty stages. Each route explains where clues come from, how to interpret them, and what to do next. Every stage also has a separate collectible gold key entry. Game artwork, screenshots, and story belong to GAMEDAY Inc. and their respective rights holders.

## Controls and useful tips

Drag the room left or right to find objects outside the current view. Inspecting, taking objects, using items, and moving are separate actions; switch to movement mode to pass through an open doorway. In the inventory, use Combine and drag one item onto another, or use Separate to unpack containers, tools, and food. Let animations finish before continuing.

Some number safes require you to pull the handle down after entering the code. Drag combination wheels until each digit sits in the middle. For hammer actions, tap the targets; for cutting actions, swipe in the indicated direction. Shop assistance items can be claimed repeatedly for free. The shop also offers confirmation to unlock every stage.

Collectible gold keys count once toward collection progress. Gold-colored keys used to unlock objects are puzzle items; use them where indicated. Screenshots help identify controls and may show a puzzle partway through, so follow the written steps for its final setting.

## Chapter 1: Memories in the room

### 1-1: The date in the photograph

1. Watch the television and listen to your wife's message. She mentions an anniversary, so look for something that records a date.
2. Take the brush from the dressing table and inspect the dusty photograph. Use the brush on the frame, then rub across the close-up until the date is visible.
3. The photograph says **08.17**. The safe needs four digits: remove the dot to get **0817**.
4. Enter the code and pull the safe handle down. Take the key, use it on the exit, open the door, and switch to movement mode to leave.

**Gold key:** On the front edge of the round foreground table, beside the diary and to the right of the photograph. Pick it up before leaving.

### 1-2: Blow the note out of the fan

1. Watch the television, then inspect the fan. Its button is missing, so it cannot start yet.
2. Take the pliers and hanger from the floor. Combine them into a hook; it can reach the object under the bed.
3. Use the hook under the bed to retrieve the fan button. Fit it to the fan and turn the fan on.
4. Wait for the blades to turn and a note to fall. Pick it up: **6614** is the safe's four-digit code.
5. Enter **6614**, pull the handle down, and use the key inside to open the exit. Leave in movement mode.

**Gold key:** Scroll right to the computer desk. It lies along the desk's front edge, to the left of the keyboard.

### 1-3: A song, its duration, and a shadow

1. Watch the television. Take the ballerina near the bed and the CD case from the shelf near the globe. Separate the case to obtain the disc and case.
2. Place the ballerina on the music box, then inspect the illuminated display. It names the song **You are my Everything**.
3. Find that song on the CD case's track list. Its duration is **03:27**, giving the four-digit safe code **0327**. Enter it, pull the handle down, and take the unusually shaped key.
4. Open the kitchen cabinet and take the hammer. Turn the wall light to the left to expose its keyhole.
5. Insert the unusual key into the light. Its shadow marks a place on the wall. Use the hammer there and complete the tapping action.
6. Take the ordinary key from the wall, unlock the exit, and leave.

**Gold key:** Open the drawer in the red kitchen cabinet at the far right. Check the drawer as well as the lower cupboard doors.

### 1-4: Release all three chains

1. The three chains on the door are linked to the television, radio, and music box. Operate the television until it turns on and releases its chain: four taps from the initial state.
2. Open the kitchen drawer, take the battery, insert it into the radio, and turn the radio on. This releases the second chain.
3. Collect glue and both broken ballerina pieces. Combine the pieces, then add glue to repair the figurine.
4. Open the music box puzzle. Rotate its rings until their artwork joins up, with the red cross upright and the darker background continuous at the bottom. Then place the repaired ballerina on it and wait for the music box and display to react, releasing the remaining chain.
5. Inspect the Beatles poster. Read the numbers on their clothes from left to right: **1, 9, 6, 3**. Enter **1963** in the safe and pull its handle down.
6. Take the key. Once all three chains are released, unlock the exit and leave in movement mode.

**Gold key:** In the fruit bowl on the kitchen table on the right. Pick it up directly.

### 1-5: Six hiding places in the diary

1. Watch the television and read both diary pages on the round table. The apple, globe, mirror, sausage, teddy bear, and picture frame point to six hiding places for the heart-shaped keys beside the door.
2. Take the kitchen knife and the hammer on the floor. Cut open the teddy bear with the knife, following the swipe prompts, and take its heart key.
3. Break the mirror, globe, and picture frame with the hammer. Each contains another heart key.
4. Take the sausage from the refrigerator and Separate it to retrieve a key. Take the apple from the table and Combine it with the knife for the final key.
5. Place all six heart keys in the matching slots beside the door. Once the mechanism opens, open the safe and take the old key.
6. Use the old key on the exit: it breaks. Complete two hammer actions on the door, open it, and pass through. Open the final door to finish the chapter.

**Gold key:** On the round foreground table, to the right of the photograph near the lamp base. Collect it separately from the six heart keys.

## Chapter 2: Following the evidence

### 2-1: Four numbers behind the blind

1. Take the hammer from the desk and smash the glass vase. Use the small key inside to unlock the drawer.
2. Take the screwdriver set. Separate it twice to obtain the star screwdriver that fits the blind's metal control box.
3. Remove the box's screws, then operate the control to open the blind. It needs two more taps after the screws are removed.
4. Record the digits appearing by the window in order: **0, 2, 1, 3**. These give the combination safe's code.
5. Set the wheels to **0213** from left to right, centering each digit. Take the key, unlock the door, and leave.

**Gold key:** Smash the small window beside the exit with the hammer and collect the key inside. This window is to the right of the large blind.

### 2-2: Reveal the message in the blood

1. Take the metal plate and police kit from the floor. Combine the kit with the bag key already in your inventory, then Separate repeatedly to obtain the instructions, sprayer, and luminol reagent.
2. Read the instructions: treated blood becomes visible in darkness. Combine the sprayer and reagent.
3. Pry open the television cabinet drawer with the metal plate and take the scissors. Cut the police tape to reach the bloodstains.
4. Spray the bloodstains, then turn off the room light. Inspect the glowing message and read **7230**; the name beside it helps identify the writing as a message.
5. Turn the light back on. Enter **7230** in the electronic lock beside the door, then open the exit and leave.

**Gold key:** Use the metal plate on the other lower drawer of the television cabinet. Check it after retrieving the scissors.

### 2-3: Follow the eyes

1. Take the utility knife and cut away the picture's covering. Inspect the picture and follow the eyes: **up, down, left, right, up, right**.
2. Enter those six directions on the arrow device to get the small key. Return to the picture if you need to watch again.
3. Use the key on both cabinet doors. Collect the drill and battery, then combine them.
4. The fireplace is the escape route, but the fire blocks it. Take the empty can, drill into the aquarium, and catch the escaping water in the can.
5. Pour the water into the fireplace. Take the stool, inspect the fireplace, and place the stool inside to reach the passage above.
6. Inspect the placed stool, then switch to movement mode and climb out.

**Gold key:** Open the wooden cabinet drawer beneath the picture on the right and collect it.

### 2-4: Skulls and three calculations

1. Inspect **6412** on the door, then cycle through all six skull pictures on the display. Each has a number: match each number to its skull's appearance.
2. Take the wrench from the right locker. Remove both bolts holding the pipe, take the pipe, and smash the door lock's glass cover.
3. On the skull buttons, select the images numbered **6 → 4 → 1 → 2**. Their positions are **bottom left, bottom right, top right, top left**. Enter the next room.
4. Take the power cord from the computer desk drawer, connect it to the socket, and turn on the monitor. Its three clue pages correspond to safes A, B, and C.
5. **Safe A:** Every row and column of the number grid must total 30. Fill the colored gaps, then read **green, red, blue, yellow**: **1, 8, 5, 6**. Enter **1856**.
6. **Safe B:** Count each of the four star shapes shown in the legend below the picture. Their totals are **7, 5, 5, 3**. Enter **7553**.
7. **Safe C:** The three numbers in each sector total 18. In green, red, blue, yellow order: 18−4−8=6; 18−5−9=4; 18−7−2=9; 18−8−6=4. Enter **6494**.
8. Take the large, medium, and small gears from the safes and fit them to the exit mechanism. Rotate them until the picture is upright and continuous, then leave.

**Gold key:** Before leaving the first room, take the screwdriver from the left locker. Remove the metal wall cover on the left and take the key inside.

### 2-5: Reassemble the switch diagram

1. Open the curtains and finish the scene. Examine and open the book, take its small key, unlock the drawer, and retrieve the double-barreled gun. Pick up the first note fragment near the fallen chair.

**Gold key:** Open locker **6** and take the key before leaving the first room.
## Chapter 3: Fingerprints and hidden passages

### 3-1: Find the buttons, then copy the fingerprint

1. Inspect the device beside the exit. It requires both button input and fingerprint recognition; the marks on its keys are too faint to read.
2. Take the brush from the pen holder and the aluminum powder bottle, then combine them. Take tape from the drawer and the ink pad from the desk.
3. Use the powdered brush on the device. In the close-up, the marked keys are **0, 1, 6, and 7**. Select those four and wait for the scan.
4. You still need the correct fingerprint. Inspect the alarm switch the boss last pressed; his print remains there.
5. Separate the tape, combine a piece with the ink pad, and use the prepared tape on the alarm switch to obtain the fingerprint.
6. Use the fingerprint tape on the reader, then open the door and leave in movement mode.

**Gold key:** On the front edge of the boss's chair seat, near the underside of the desk. Pick it up directly.

### 3-2: Count the projected colors

![Circuit connections](assets/circuit.png)

1. Take the hammer under the sofa, break the DVD cabinet glass, and retrieve the battery. Combine it with the remote on the coffee table and use the remote on the wall projector.
2. Watch the four-colored projection. Follow the color order shown on the wall and count each color's sections: **blue 4, yellow 5, red 4, green 5**. This gives **4545**.
3. The safe is behind the screen, whose control has no power. Take the screwdriver from the right drawer, remove the circuit box screws, and open its cover.
4. Connect neighboring circuit dots from the initially lit dot: **right, up, right, right, right, right, down, left, left, left, down, right, right, down, right, up, right, up, right, up**. Wait for each lighting animation before selecting the next dot.
5. Operate the restored screen control to raise the screen. Enter **4545** in the safe, pull its handle down, and take the key.
6. Unlock the exit and leave.

**Gold key:** Beneath the glass on the right side of the coffee table. Break that glass with the hammer before picking up the key.

### 3-3: Numbers in the gaps of the score

1. Inspect the wall score and drag horizontally to see all of it. The empty spaces between notes and connecting lines form digits. Read in the arrow's direction to get **4710**.
2. Enter **4710** on the tablet device, then take the gunpowder and fuse from the opened compartment. Search the drawers for blue tape and a lighter.
3. Open the refrigerator and take the ice containing a key. Take the tweezers near the stove. The ice must melt before you can retrieve the key.
4. Put the ice in the glass kettle and turn on its burner. After it melts, use the tweezers to take the key from the hot water.
5. In the inventory, combine gunpowder with the fuse, then add blue tape to make this stage's explosive prop. Attach it to the kitchen exit and use the lighter to trigger the door-opening scene.
6. Return through the broken doorway to the corridor. Use the recovered key on the dressing-room door to continue.

**Gold key:** Inside the refrigerator. Pick it up while collecting the ice.

### 3-4: From BLUE SHIRT to VASE

![Watch puzzle](assets/watch.png)

1. Take the note from the dressing-room floor: **4, 1, 8, 12 / 9, 7, 5, 2, 10**. Compare the floor pattern with the central door's nine-square panel.
2. Light **top left, top right, center, and bottom right** to match the floor. Enter the study through the hidden passage.
3. Take the knife and inspect the numbered book spines. Use the note's order: 4=B, 1=L, 8=U, 12=E; 9=S, 7=H, 5=I, 2=R, 10=T. This spells **BLUE SHIRT**.
4. Return to the dressing room and cut the blue shirt. Use its small key on the central cabinet to reveal four watches.
5. Inspect the compass and wall clock in the study. The compass's red needle means north; the clock's red hand points toward five o'clock. Treat that direction as north to interpret the watch hands as **W, S, N, E**.
6. Set the watch letters to **W, S, N, E**. From their initial state, tap the four controls **1, 2, 3, and 0 times**. The hands then move to **3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock**.
7. Look those numbers up on the book spines: 3=V, 6=A, 9=S, 12=E. Enter **VASE** in the letter safe and take the vase.
8. Inspect the vase and Separate it to retrieve its trapped key. Return to the corridor and unlock the living room.

**Gold key:** Cut the folded clothes on the open shelf on the dressing room's right. The blue shirt contains the ordinary cabinet key; search both places.

### 3-5: Colored lights, a cork, and the window

![Four marks on the wine cork](assets/clues/wine-cork.png)

![Wine cabinet puzzle](assets/wine.png)

1. Take the hammer from the living-room table. The scene points to the window as your escape, but you need to raise the blind, break the glass, and prepare a long rope.
2. Return through the corridor to the kitchen. Take the knife and corkscrew, and cut down the curtain. In the dressing room, take the pliers from the drawer, the metal hanger and diamond necklace from the shelf, and cut down that curtain too.
3. Enter the study through the hidden passage and cut down its curtain. Back in the living room, combine two curtains, then add the third to make the sturdy rope.
4. Combine the pliers and hanger into a short wire. The opening scene has unlocked the fuse box on the left wall: open it by hand and put the wire in the missing fuse position.
5. Turn on the auxiliary light, then turn off the main light. Observe the colored pattern in the dark, including the outer ring and middle lights. Turn the main light back on afterward.
6. Copy that layout on the wine cabinet. Starting at the top and going clockwise, set the outer lights **alternately green and red**. Set both inner lights **green**. Lights cycle off → red → green; the center button resets them.
7. Take the red wine from the opened cabinet and combine it with the corkscrew. The cork's four vertical marks read **1111**. The blind control says **RED WINE**, linking it to this clue; enter **1111**.
8. Once the blind rises, score the glass with the diamond necklace, then break it with the hammer.
9. Attach the sturdy rope at the window and leave along it in movement mode.

**Gold key:** Inside the narrow-necked glass vase to the left of the living-room bar. Smash the vase and collect the exposed key.

## Chapter 4: Messages in the ruins

### 4-1: Follow the red and blue ball diagram

1. Take the hammer, break the rock, and press the revealed button. Inspect all four alien figures in the resulting mural to open the UFO compartment on the left.
2. Take its bundle of blue balls and Separate repeatedly until you have all six.
3. Inspect the stone tablet on the right. Six blue balls mark the outer points of a six-pointed star; four red balls mark the corners of its inner square.
4. Place the blue balls in the six outer holes: top, bottom, upper left, upper right, lower left, lower right. Keep the four inner holes free.
5. The four alien murals change. Break each with the hammer, collect four red balls, and place them in the inner square's holes.
6. The completed pattern raises the pendant from the floor. Put it in the central wall position marked **Pendant**, wait for the stone door, and leave.

**Gold key:** Hammer the small glowing wall mark just right of the UFO compartment on the left side of the room. Take the key from the broken brick.

### 4-2: Convert 1324 into brick positions

![Pyramid puzzle](assets/pyramid.png)

1. Take the hammer and golden airplane. Fit the airplane into the matching recess in the right UFO compartment and take the plug-shaped fossil.

**Gold key:** Appears among the fragments at the sphinx's base after the second hammer action. Collect it along with the Earth model.

### 4-3: Use the water level to open the stone door

1. Take the hammer and inspect the tablet. It shows a broken moon, then a T-shaped support and prism: your first steps.

6. Add two more buckets of water, keeping the water in the tube this time. Take the floating moon model and put it in the moon slot.
7. Watch the clock as the tube fills: at full water it points to **11 o'clock**, giving **1100** as a four-digit time. On the glowing safe, slide to each digit and tap to enter **1100**.

**Gold key:** Under the floor tile at the far right, in front of the UFO compartment. Break it with the hammer. This is separate from the drain-button tile.

### 4-4: Gear revolutions and skeleton directions

1. Take the hammer and gear 1 from the floor. Read **BREAK ME** on the left tablet, break it, and enter the passage.
2. Take the golden airplane and read every page of the old book. The first marks three hiding places; the second gives gear revolutions **3, 6, 4, 10**. Later pages show planets and a human pose.
3. Back in the main room, fit the airplane to the UFO compartment and open the timer. Hold the right button for a duration within its displayed range. Initially this is **11–14 seconds**; aim for about **12.5 seconds**. A failed attempt changes the range, so read it again.
4. Take the pyramid tip and fit it to the pyramid in the passage. Three circles light up. Follow the book's marks and hammer the gem positions between the lights to collect the remaining three gears.
5. Gears 1, 2, 3, 4 have **20, 15, 10, 6 teeth**. Match them to the book's revolutions: 20×3=10×6=15×4=6×10=60. Install gears **1, 3, 2, 4** from left to right.
6. Rotate each activated disc group to match the skeleton below it. Following this route from the initial state takes **4, 5, 2, 7 taps**. If you have already moved them, use the matching directions instead.
7. Take the crystal from the floor and insert it into the exit's crystal-shaped recess. Leave through the opened stone door.

**Gold key:** Appears at the bottom of the doorway once the crystal is installed and the door opens. Take it before leaving.

### 4-5: Translate the symbols into the final message

1. Inspect the first room's tablet. Its upper section shows four skeleton types and aligned planets; below, eight symbols correspond to notes from low to high.
2. Enter the previous gear room and align the planets on all four disc groups. From the initial state, tap each group **three times**. Collect four skeletons from the opened floor.
3. Return and place them in tablet order: **ape, human, giant, alien**. Tap the illuminated central sphere and read the score.
4. Translate each note through the tablet's pitch-to-symbol mapping. Number the eight buttons 1–8 from left to right. The first score is **1, 2, 3, 1, 3, 1, 3, 2, 3, 4, 4, 3, 2, 4**. Wait between inputs, then tap the central sphere again when finished.
5. Collect the items revealed on eight pedestals. The Statue of Liberty ahead needs a book and torch.
6. Make the book: **tissue + cup of water → wet paper; wet paper + square mold → paper block; paper block + cover → book**. Make the torch: **rod + cloth → simple torch; add wire; add the lighter to light it**.
7. Enter the third room and put the book and torch in the statue's matching positions to activate the door puzzle.
8. In each round, inspect and operate the snake head to reveal the current picture, then open the letter device. Enter **PENDANT**, **EARTH**, **ALIEN**, **CRYSTAL**, and **ENGLISH** for the successive clues. They match earlier objects, labels, and the language clue.
9. The first letters of those five answers spell **P E A C E**: enter **PEACE** in the final round. Slide to each letter and tap to enter it, letting each round's animation finish. Leave through the final door.

**Gold key:** On the floor beside the skeleton in the right foreground of the second, gear-filled room. Collect it while taking the four skeletons.
## Chapter 5: Museum mechanisms

### 5-1: Return the genuine painting

1. Take the testing reagent and toolbox from the floor. Inspect the paintings: their circles and colored marks indicate the color wheel's orientation. *The Wedding at Cana* has lost its colors.
2. Apply the reagent to *The Wedding at Cana* to reveal colored figures. Remember this painting; you will count them later.
3. Separate the toolbox repeatedly for its handle and sockets. Combine the handle with the square socket and fit it to the square wall fitting.
4. Rotate the color wheel to match the paintings' marks: **green upper left, blue upper right, red lower left, yellow lower right**. This takes **7 taps** from the initial state. A wooden board appears and the display case's keypad activates.
5. The board names *The Wedding at Cana* and shows **yellow, blue, red, green, purple, orange**. Count its figures in that order: **6, 4, 5, 9, 3, 3**. Enter **645933** at the case.

### 5-3: OLYMPIC and the numbered shelves

2. Once the elevator opens, the glass door shows overlapping **PUSH / PULL** letters. Read the word facing you and enter **PUSH** in the letter device.
3. Enter the final hall and listen to the floor-puzzle warning. The glowing symbol on the far door resembles a capital **I**; reproduce it on the floor.
4. In movement mode, count rows from the top. Step on all five tiles of row 1, the middle tile of row 2, the middle tile of row 3, and all five tiles of row 4.
5. The result is two horizontal bars joined by a central vertical line. Collect the gold key after the exit opens, then leave.

**Gold key:** Appears near the open exit after completing the floor pattern. Switch back to taking mode to collect it before passing through.

## Chapter 6

### 6-1: Turn the pipe into a lever

1. Inspect the wooden door's gap. A catch outside is beyond your hand's reach. Take and Separate the wire bundle, then use the long bent wire to remove the catch.
2. Open the door by hand and enter the workshop in movement mode. The main door is chained; inspect the floor hatch and its narrow gap on the left.
3. Take the heavy iron block, ordinary pipe, and backpack. Separate the backpack repeatedly for a flashlight, rope, and portable knife.
4. The round pipe end will not fit the gap, so flatten it. Pry open the wall switch box with the knife and place the pipe on the steel floor plate beneath the hanging weight.
5. Press the switch to lower the weight and flatten one end of the pipe. The processed pipe is added to your inventory.
6. The pipe alone gives insufficient leverage. Put the heavy iron block beside the hatch's left edge as a fulcrum, then use the processed pipe on the hatch to pry it open.
7. Collect the gold key, switch to movement mode, and descend through the hatch.

**Gold key:** On the small cabinet to the right of the chained door, below the small screen. It is available as soon as you enter the workshop.

### 6-2: Find the parts and rebuild the machine

1. Tap the small ceiling bulb to turn on the light. Investigate the person behind the boxes and finish the conversation. Open the wall control device to see its missing parts.
2. Collect exposed parts **2, 4, 5, and 8** from the central floor, floor in front of the sofa, lower-left floor, and area in front of the floor cabinets.
3. Open the small wall cabinet for **part 1**, the left floor cabinet for **9**, and the right floor cabinet for **6**. Lift the sofa cloth for **7**.
4. Take the hammer. Something shines through the boarded area on the right: break the boards and take **part 3**. You should now have all nine parts.
5. Use all nine on the opened control device, then tap it to enter the fitting puzzle. Drag the loose parts on the right into the left recess. Correct placement snaps into place; misplaced pieces return.
6. Match their shapes: the corner piece with two white cylinders goes at the upper left; the zigzag linkage below it; the narrow piston at upper center; the yellow square at middle left; the large perforated part at lower center; the other corner linkage at lower left.
7. Build the right strip from top to bottom with the turbine, three-hole T-shaped linkage, and bulb corner piece. Align edges against the two fixed pieces already in the recess. Completing the assembly lights the exit and opens its door.
8. Finish the dialogue, collect the gold key, and leave through the right exit.

**Gold key:** After turning on the light, look high on the left wall, below the red light near the left end of the horizontal pipe. Pick it up by hand.

### 6-3: Reverse the mirrored note

![Fuse note, front](assets/clues/fuse-note-front.png)

![Mirrored fuse note](assets/clues/fuse-note-back.png)

1. Take the hammer, both floor notes, and exposed fuses **1 and 3**. Lift the floor cloth for **2**, open the left cabinet for **4**, and hammer the stuck drawer for **5**.
2. Compare the notes. The first has normal “open device” writing and dots at upper left, upper right, and lower left. The second's writing is mirrored, so reverse its colored dots horizontally too.
3. The corrected colors are **red upper left, blue upper right, yellow lower left**. Fuse **1 is red, 3 blue, and 4 yellow**. Install them in those slots, leaving the center and lower right empty.
4. Press the device button to enable the safe below. Inspect the television: the person in the news holds a plate numbered **9764**, the safe code.
5. Enter **9764** at the safe and pull its handle. Take the device key.
6. Enter the adjacent room, use the key in the mechanism near the lever, and pull the lever once its light comes on. After hearing the door open next door, return.
7. Collect the gold key and leave through the now-open exit.

**Gold key:** On the first room's foreground floor beside scattered papers, below and left of the long control panel. You can take it before opening the safe.

### 6-4: Freeze the lock before breaking it

1. Remove the central machine's cloth to discover a cooling device. Take the hammer, handle, and empty bottle. Open the right drawer for the old key.
2. Try the old key on the exit lock: it breaks. Inspect the cooler; rust prevents its nozzles from turning.
3. Fit the handle to the large oil drum's valve and turn it. Catch the flowing lubricant in the empty bottle.
4. Apply the lubricant to the rusty cooler so its three nozzles can turn. Aim all three cooling jets at the exit lock.
5. From the state immediately after oiling, leave the top nozzle alone, tap the **middle nozzle twice and bottom nozzle once**, then press the start button.
6. The jets freeze the lock. Smash it with the hammer, open the door by hand, and leave in movement mode.

**Gold key:** High in the room near the horizontal ceiling pipe above the central machine. Tap the small gold key beside the pipe.

### 6-5: Restore power and reach the last passage

1. Take the oxygen cylinder, gas cylinder, cutter, utility knife, and hook. Open both desk drawers for two pencils.
2. The emergency generator cover is firmly attached. Combine the cutter with the oxygen cylinder, then the gas cylinder, to make the working torch.
3. Cut open the generator cover and press the exposed start button by hand.
4. Inspect the two broken wire connections on the wall. Graphite from the pencils can conduct electricity in this puzzle. Combine the knife with each pencil to obtain two graphite cores.
5. Fit **core 1 to the right connection and core 2 to the left**. Once power is restored, pass through the door into the next room.
6. Take the key from the floor and rope beside the pillar. Combine the rope and hook. Inspect the ceiling passage and use the hooked rope at its attachment point.
7. First open the small cabinet on the left wall and collect the final gold key. Then climb the rope in movement mode.
8. Use the ordinary key on the final lock, open the door by hand, and pass through to finish your escape.

**Gold key:** In the second room's small left-wall cabinet above the console. Open it by hand. The ordinary key from the floor is for the final door.
